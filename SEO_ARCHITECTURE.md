# SEO architecture notes

## Benchmarked patterns
Current leading crypto/finance publishers commonly combine:
- category hubs for exchanges / apps / wallets;
- "best" and comparison intent content;
- individual platform reviews;
- transparent review methodology;
- educational supporting articles;
- clear discussion of fees, security, product availability and user fit;
- internal links between informational and commercial pages.

TopPick.pro preserves those **patterns** without copying competitor wording or page text.

## Planned clusters
- `/exchanges` → exchange reviews → exchange-vs-exchange comparisons → fee/security/beginner guides
- `/brokers` → broker reviews → broker-vs-exchange → platform/instrument/risk guides
- `/wallets` → wallet reviews → hot-vs-cold / hardware comparisons → self-custody education
- `/markets` → country/province/state pages only when evidence is complete
- `/learn` → evergreen supporting education

## YMYL / finance quality controls
Crypto and leveraged trading content can influence financial decisions. Production pages should use current primary sources, visible freshness dates, authorship/editorial policy, clear risk language, transparent affiliate disclosure and jurisdiction-aware claims.
